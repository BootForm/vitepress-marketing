# 3 things we check before every quote

*CHANGE ME: another example post. Two posts, linked from `blog/index.md`, is enough to show the
pattern; delete both and write your own whenever you're ready.*

A quote that changes once we show up isn't a quote, it's a guess with a number attached. Three
things we always check first, before we tell you a price:

1. **Soil, not just the lawn.** A patchy lawn is usually a soil problem, not a mowing problem.
   Fixing the wrong thing costs you twice.
2. **Drainage.** Standing water after rain changes what we recommend planting, and where.
3. **What's already there.** Ripping out a healthy hedge to start over is rarely the cheapest or
   the best option, even when it looks that way at first glance.

None of this changes the price we quote you. It changes whether the price we quote is the price
you actually pay.
